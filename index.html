Root Cause Diagnostics: Post-processing of the simulation data uncovers a major hydraulic constraint. Squeezing the coolant through an expansive serpentine routing footprint chokes the net flow area down to 2e-05 m², introducing massive fluid resistance. Consequently, the mass flow rate drops to an under-provisioned 0.000144 kg/s, allowing localized energy to flash component temperatures to critical thresholds.

Engineering Recommendation: Migrate from a serial serpentine path to a parallel microchannel distribution manifold. Increasing the parallel flow cross-section path minimizes system loop resistance, scaling fluid mass velocity up significantly to bring hot boundary zones under control.
